Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

This policy covers Picberry for macOS and this website. Picberry processes your photo library locally; visiting this website or contacting support involves the separate services described below.

For privacy questions, contact milanvandal@gmail.com.

Your photos, videos, and metadata

Picberry works with the libraries and files you choose to make available to it. Media and associated metadata are processed on your Mac for browsing, organization, search, playback, and generated memories. Metadata can include file names, capture dates, camera details, and location coordinates already embedded in your media.

Picberry stores library indexes, thumbnails, previews, settings, tags, albums, and other generated data on your Mac and, where applicable, alongside your library. Picberry does not require a Picberry account or upload your library to a Picberry cloud service. If you keep a library in a folder synchronized by another service, that service controls its synchronization.

Local image analysis and People recognition

Image analysis, including Magic Tags and People recognition, runs on your Mac. People recognition models are included with the app. Photos and analysis results are not sent to an online AI service.

People recognition starts when you enable it. Picberry uses detected faces and numerical face representations to group photos of the same person. Those results and any names you add remain in local Picberry data. Turning recognition off stops further background analysis; it does not erase people already identified.

Permissions and device connections

You choose the folders and files Picberry can access. Picberry remembers authorized library access so you can reopen a library on a later launch. If access is unavailable, the app may ask you to grant it again.

When you use local transfer or casting features, Picberry communicates with the devices involved and transfers the media you choose over that connection. These features may require local network access. The receiving device and its manufacturer's services operate under their own privacy policies.

Diagnostics

Picberry keeps diagnostic error logs locally to help troubleshoot problems. Logs rotate as they grow. The released app does not automatically upload these logs or include product analytics, advertising trackers, or an automatic crash-report upload service. You can choose to share diagnostics with support using Copy Logs in Settings.

Keeping and removing local data

Library data remains locally while you use that library, unless you clear or remove it. You can clear generated previews in Settings or remove a library from Picberry. Removing a library deletes its exclusive app-local catalog, caches, memories, and People data.

Original photos and videos and completed imports are preserved when you remove a library. Picberry also attempts to remove its validated generated files from an accessible, writable source library. Generated files on an unavailable, read-only, or unverified source may remain. Your operating system, backups, and any services you use to synchronize files have their own storage and deletion behavior.

Support messages

If you email milanvandal@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. These are used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please share only the information needed for support.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and maintain relevant support records. You can use the same email address to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent. Email is handled through the email providers used by you and the developer, which have their own privacy and retention practices.

Purchases and Apple services

App purchases and updates are handled by Apple through the Mac App Store. Picberry does not ask for payment-card details in the app. Apple's processing of purchase, account, and platform information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

This website

This website has no contact form, accounts, advertising, or visitor analytics added by Picberry. Its pages do not set cookies or load third-party fonts or embedded media.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without a GitHub account. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's handling of hosting data.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Picberry or its data practices change. The current policy and its effective date will be published on this page. For questions or requests about information handled by the developer, email milanvandal@gmail.com.
