Compatibility

Picberry for macOS requires macOS 12 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

Report a problem

Include your Picberry version, macOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Add the exact error message if one appears. Please avoid sending private photos or other sensitive information unless they are needed to investigate the issue.

Common questions

My library is unavailable

Reconnect the drive containing the library. If Picberry asks for access, select Allow Access and choose the original library folder. Check that the files are available in Finder.

Previews are missing or out of date

Check that the source drive is connected and its media files are available. Open Settings and select Update Cache. Keep the drive connected while Picberry rebuilds its generated previews.

How do I start or stop People recognition?

Select Start Recognition in People, or enable People recognition in Settings. Picberry processes photos in the background on your Mac. Turn the setting off to stop further analysis. People already found remain available.

Will removing a library delete my photos?

Removing a library from Picberry preserves original media and completed imports. It removes the library from the app and deletes its exclusive local Picberry data. Some generated files may remain on a source drive that is unavailable, read-only, or cannot be verified.

How do I send diagnostic logs?

If support requests a log, open Settings and select Copy Logs, then paste the copied log file into your email. Review the file before sending it. Copying a log does not send it automatically.
